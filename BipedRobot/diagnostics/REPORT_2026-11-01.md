# Biped status report, 2026-11-01 evening

Written for the project owner and for the next working session. Details and raw numbers: `diagnostics/PROGRESS.md`. All results are in simulation; nothing here shows real-world validity or paper readiness.

Training is now about 20 times faster, so experiments that took 20 hours take about an hour. That was the largest blocker for the roadmap. The robot moves forward reliably, but it is not smooth and not straight.

## Proven (measured)

- **Speed:** SB3 15 updates/s; GPU replay alone 44; fused Adam no gain; CUDA graph 251 (about 290 per run with 2 fast and 2 SB3 runs). 1M steps in 57 min.
- **Regression:** no detectable learning loss, SB3 vs fast, seeds 3+4: easy 0.2 53/60 vs 57/60, easy 0.3 50/60 vs 51/60, medium 13/60 vs 16/60, full+pushes 0/60 vs 2/60, standing 58/60 vs 53/60. n=2 seeds per arm, so this is not proof of equivalence.
- **Determinism:** fixed seed gives identical parameters after about 1,000 updates.
- **Full-realism training:** same config and seed as A-min s4: full+pushes 17/30 vs 0/30; medium 28/30 vs 9/30; easy 0/30 vs 28/30.
- **Reward family:** A-min and Smooth-A do not differ in survival. Training on the full command range gave no benefit.
- **Hopping:** 2 hop episodes in 480 across checkpoints and speeds. Shaped policies walk with about 1% flight.
- **Budget buffer:** `her/budget_her_replay_buffer.py` is broken under worker processes and p_budget_relabel does not work. No recorded run used it.
- **Checkpoint swings:** survival at easy 0.2 changes a lot between nearby checkpoints. Turn run: 18/30 at 400k, 0/30 at 500k (running at 0.375 m/s). Turn + smooth: 12/30 at 400k, 24/30 at 500k (0.113 m/s). Single-checkpoint comparisons are unreliable, including the regression, which used final checkpoints only.
- **Motion quality:** commands jump by 0.46-0.83 per 20 ms step, torque limit 39-62% of the time, no policy walks straight unaided. Fast s4 ends 0.37 m off line, curving left +21+-10 deg.

## Hypotheses (not tested)

- The full-realism policy depends on the actuator delay and lag it trained with.
- It undershoots speed because the tracking reward barely punishes it: at sigma_v 0.15, walking 0.13 for a 0.2 command loses only about 10% of the tracking reward. A tighter sigma_v may fix speed.
- Gait shaping, not HER, is what prevents hopping.
- A torque penalty is the strongest missing piece for smoothness.
- The smoothing settings make motion calmer: at 400k, sway 0.155 vs 0.213 m/s and torque limit 39-41% vs 61%. Two settings changed together, so the cause is not separated. Their effect on survival is unknown: see checkpoint swings under Proven.

## Unknown

- Whether full-realism results replicate across seeds.
- Whether a policy walks in full realism with gait shaping off.
- Whether smoothing settings work under actuator lag.
- Whether any setup reaches 0.15-0.25 m/s with at least 90% survival in medium.
- Whether hopping appears before a fall in the unclassified episodes under 3 s.
- Whether budget-HER can replace hand-tuned shaping. This is the paper's main claim.

## Risks

- **Compute:** `generate.py` (about 9 cores, 7 GB) and PID 1604315 (about 11 cores) leave 0% CPU idle. Neither is touched.
- **Memory:** available memory has reached 7-8 GB. Paused runs still hold memory. Long jobs must use setsid after a session restart killed the first grid.
- **E-B design:** "lower flight than A-min" has almost nothing to beat. Compare against shaped and unshaped baselines instead.

## Decisions

Given broad approval on 2026-11-01 to do what seems best:

1. Stop the turn and smooth SB3 runs. They are off-roadmap, hold about 12 GB, and can be rerun in about an hour each. Their checkpoints to 500k stay on disk.
2. Drop the remaining 15 UTD grid cells; let the 2 running cells finish. E-B already fits in the available compute.
3. Install the FastSAC options into `fast/` (default behaviour verified identical by parameter digest).
4. Run 9 fast runs in full realism, 1M steps, seeds 3, 4, 5 chosen in advance, final checkpoint only, tested in medium on fresh seeds:
   - shaped: `pilot_gait_min_v2`
   - no gait shaping: `variant_min_nogait`
   - smoothing: `variant_min_smooth`
5. Keep the SB3 seed 3 replication and no-gait ablation running as clean comparisons.
6. Because of the checkpoint swings, E-A runs are also tested at 800k and 900k (the gate still reads the final checkpoint only), and the SB3-vs-fast regression is rechecked at 800k and 900k.
