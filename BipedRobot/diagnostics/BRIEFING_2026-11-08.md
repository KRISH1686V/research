## The main findings

**1. The legs barely bend: this is a stiff-legged waddle.** Measured at the top of each swing, against the 0.2 s before lift-off (4 episodes, command 0.05, heuristic tool):

| Policy | Foot lift mm | Hip on that side rises mm | Leg shortens, hip to foot, mm |
|---|---|---|---|
| r3combo s12 | 32.4 | 23.4 | 3.4 |
| r3c30 s11 | 20.3 | 11.1 | 2.8 |
| combo s13 | 20.0 | 13.8 | 2.6 |
| robustcom s12 | 15.5 | 16.6 | -0.2 |
| r3combo s11 | 14.1 | 14.3 | 0.4 |

So 55-107% of the lift comes from the whole body tipping. The swing-height reward measures from the floor, so tipping earns it. Asking for more lift bought more rock, which is why rock grew with lift across seeds. Every earlier fix was treating a symptom of this.

**2. My targets were wrong.** They had no step length or foot spacing, and tested speed only up to 0.05 m/s. That is how 1.8 cm steps with feet 147 mm apart scored 31 of 32.

**3. Foot spacing was uncontrolled:** 52 to 147 mm across policies (standing 104 mm; the designer's styles 60-90 mm).

**4. Upright reward width.** In round 3 I narrowed roll width from 8 to 5 deg, weight 0.5. At a 20-28 deg rock its slope is 0.000-0.012 per deg at the peak, so it gave no signal. Now 10 deg, weight 1.0, about 0.07 per deg.

**5. Delay is the main robustness factor.** At 40 ms, policies trained at 0-20 ms stay up 82-96% (combo), 84-94% (r3combo), 67% (cleanflat2), 51% (stride16). Those trained at 0-40 ms stay up 99-100%.

**6. Adding things breaks the gait, sometimes.** Full robust base + round 3 fixes at 2 steps/s: lift 1-7 mm, taps 2-5/s, on both seeds. The same at 3 steps/s worked. r3combo + delay 0-40 ms alone did not collapse (s11 lift 8.5 mm, rock 13; s12 lift 24.1 mm, rock 25.3), so delay is not the cause. **Cause not determined.** Training pushes of 2-9 N or 1-5 N also ruined the gait.

**7. Seeds split widely:** r3combo lift 9-13 vs 22-26 mm, r4c20d 8.5 vs 24 mm, spacing 90 vs 147 mm. With 2 seeds per arm, any pass is an early sign only.

**8. Pushes.** One 0.1 s push, survived out of 80 at 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 N: clean-gait policy 80 / 42 / 7 / 0. Weak, not yet a priority.

## Weak points you should challenge

- The reward now has about 15 hand-tuned gait terms, and each round adds more. The method's budgets are not what is shaping this walk. Budgets-off has not been run on it, so what the budgets contribute here is not determined. The paper still needs that comparison, plus Lagrangian, fixed-penalty, episode-budget, PPO and 5-seed runs.
- The leg-bend reward is untested beyond code tests and a CPU smoke run. Stiff-legged waddling is a strong local optimum, and bending costs joint speed: at 0.1 m/s, 2 steps/s there is little margin below the 80% speed term (4.24 rad/s).
- Only the simulator's servo model stands behind any of this. Loaded servo behaviour is unmeasured.
- Fixes to date were chosen by me from seeing symptoms, and two of them (roll width, target list) were wrong.
