# Biped gait: results since the 6 Nov plan, 7 Nov 2026 evening

This follows the 6 Nov briefing and the plan it produced. It is self-contained.

## Goal now

A 12-joint hobby-servo biped (AsterisCrack V2, 1.66 kg, Feetech STS3215 at 12 V, 50 Hz policy on a Raspberry Pi) must walk on the real robot by **Tuesday 10 Nov**. Robot tests start Monday 9 Nov.

The owner's asks, in order:
1. A straight path.
2. A clean step: lift one foot, place it, lift the other, place it.
3. About 2 steps per second.
4. An upright torso.
5. Higher lift, longer steps, and as much speed as the servos allow.

All results below are from the MuJoCo simulator unless marked otherwise.

## Setup (unchanged unless noted)

**Learning**
- SAC with hindsight relabeling (HER), 8 envs, 7.2M steps per run. About 4–4.7 h per run when 4 run at once.
- The goal is a velocity command [vx, vy, wz] plus 3 token budgets (flight, slip, torque).
- The critic sees privileged simulator state; left-right mirror augmentation is on.

**Actor inputs (all measurable on the robot)**
- 5 frames of joint angles, joint speeds from angle differences, gyro, gravity and the previous action.
- The command and budgets.
- A gait clock (sin and cos).
- The mean yaw rate over one cycle.

**Action:** joint-target offsets through a low-pass filter.

**Servo model, fitted to the real robot on 6 Nov** (hanging, unloaded)
- Hidden acceleration cap cleared.
- Delay 0–20 ms, lag 10 ms, backlash 0.2°.
- Torque cap 2.94 N·m, no-load speed 5.3 rad/s.
- Training assumes full servo torque. The owner will set the robot's driver to 1000 of 1000.

**Real robot so far:** one ground test of the old reward-free policy. It walked on its toes, took too many steps, leaned forward and fell without support, and curved. Which checkpoint, and whether the cap was cleared, are not known.

## What was done with the 6 Nov plan (and corrections found in the code)

**Corrections to the briefing and the plan**
- **The "alive bonus 2.5" in the config is never read.** The bonus in use is 0.2 per step, in both real and relabeled samples.
- **Torque saturation is 0–1% for every policy,** not 41–62%. The 41–62 figure was joint-acceleration percentiles.
- **The frozen shared reward adds the gait terms only when |goal velocity| > 0.1 m/s.** Below that it pays −0.3 per touchdown instead. So at the plan's slow commands every gait term would have been off and stepping punished. Fixed with a new option, `gait_always`, that copies the gait terms into the slow branch.
- **HER relabeling of [vx, vy, wz]: confirmed.** At command 0.1, curved walks under the relabeled goal earn 111–275% of the tracking reward a straight walk earns under its real goal. Fixed: only vx and the budgets are relabeled now; vy and wz keep the stored 0.

**New options in the project package (each off unless named, each unit-tested)**

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| `gait_always` | Gait terms apply at every command speed. |
| `straight` | Reward for heading and sideways offset from the start (simulator truth); both errors are also critic inputs. |
| `swing_contact` | Penalty while a foot carries over 5% of body weight during its clock swing, except near each change of feet. |
| `phase_margin` | Both feet down near a change of feet counts as matching the clock. |
| `foot_flat.free_ends` | Heel strike and toe-off tilt are free near a change of feet; the full tilt penalty applies in mid-stance. |
| `raw_action_rate` | Penalty on the policy's own output change, before the filter. |
| `upright` | Reward 0.5·exp(−(pitch/4°)² − (roll/8°)²). The old posture term was about 2e-4 at 10°. |
| `duty_range` | Servo power randomization. Built, not used, since the owner will run at full torque. |

## Results

**How they were measured:** 7.2M steps, 2 seeds per arm (11 and 12). 100 test episodes of 10 s each, on seeds 9001–9100, which played no part in training or selection. Medium realism, mid budget, full servo power, heading hold off. Every arm uses hand-written gait rewards, so all results are "shaped".

**Every arm, every seed, both commands:**
- stays up 98–100%;
- flight at most 0.2%;
- straight: 0.10–0.23 rad of turn and 1–12 cm of sideways drift per 10 s.

**Other measurements**
- **Clean at half servo power:** stays up 93–100% (one seed 79% in place).
- **Clean at command 0.1** (above its training range): whole sole 39–62%, taps 0.3–1.1/s.
- **Heading hold:** changes nothing.
- **Earlier reward-free and old-style policies** (for comparison): 3–6 steps/s, toe or edge stance 65–99%, turn up to 1.4 rad per 10 s.

## What the results show (my reading; please challenge it)

1. **The clean gait works:** straight, about 2 steps/s, one stance per foot per cycle, toe-walking gone, no falls.
2. **Flat foot and lift trade off.** Doubling the flat-foot penalty fixed the sole (92–95%) and cost lift (18 mm).
3. **Smoothing raised lift but hurt posture.** Lift went to 22–24 mm and pitch spread to −10..+5°. The output change fell only 10–20%.
4. **Torso pitch is the common miss.** It drifts by seed and command: leaning back when stepping in place, forward at speed. No reward held it until `upright` (now training).
5. **Allowing double support doesn't produce it.** Both feet are down only 0.2–1.1% of the time; nothing rewards it.
6. **Speed and lift each reach their target in the arm built for them,** but not together. That follows the kinematic table: higher lift needs a slower step, and more speed needs heel-to-toe.

## Training now

**combo**, seeds 11–14 (4 seeds), results about midnight. It is cleanbase plus:
- the flat-foot penalty ×2;
- filter 0.2 and raw rate 0.05;
- `upright` (weight 0.5, pitch 4°, roll 8°);
- stepping in place 15%;
- stricter speed kernel (v_rel 0.4, v_min 0.03);
- `phase_margin` 0.05.

Commands 0.03–0.07 m/s at 2 steps/s. Pass lines were set beforehand: the targets from the 6 Nov plan, plus speed ≥ 0.04 m/s and both feet down 5–15% at 0.05 m/s, on 3 of the 4 seeds.

## Constraints

- At most 4 training runs at once, on one GPU shared through MPS. About 4.5 h per 7.2M-step run under the current load. No resume.
- The core environment, trainer and evaluation files are frozen. Changes go in as options (off by default) or new configs in the project's own package.
- The actor sees only what the robot measures; the critic and rewards may use simulator truth.
- Servo limits: about 5.3 rad/s no-load, 2.94 N·m.
- Results with gait rewards are reported apart from reward-free ones. A claim needs fixed seeds and fresh test seeds; 2 seeds is an early sign, 3 is a result so far, and 5 supports a conclusion.
- Out of scope: servo heating models, a separate price head, MJX/Playground/Warp.
- The paper's method claim (budget-as-goal HER) rests on the reward-free result: fixed goal met on 5 of 5 seeds, budgets off clearly worse. The shaped walk still needs a budgets-off comparison, 5 seeds, and real-robot results.
